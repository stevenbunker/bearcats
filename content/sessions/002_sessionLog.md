## Log

We went in search of [[Giselle]] to the north. [[Thimble Bramblewood]] attempted to talk to an alligator to see if the tattered clothing in front of it belonged to our inquiry. [[Thorne Wilder]] grew tired and shot it with his bow. After a brief fight, the alligator was nearly dead; hanging on by a thread. That's when Thimble summoned all the power of the moon to completely obliterate the animal, the contents of the animals stomach, and the value of the animal's leather. Fortunately there was just enough left behind to discern the animal may have bitten the arm off a lizard-folk.

We continued down the path and found a group of lizard folk around a fire, one missing an arm. [[Giovanni Vittoro]] chatted them up, and was able to lure 3 of them away from the others. Thimble trapped them in an entangle spell.
