# [[player characters/Nedri Tallflower.md|Nedri Tallflower]]

## Background

On the night of the Red Moon theft, the [[Iron Eclipse Kabul]] descended on the town for the [[Ring of Invisibility]] suppressing sound and scent. They killed Nedri's whole family while Nedri hid in the fireplace.

Since then, Nedri has trained as a rogue. [[Kale Blackbain]], Nedri's underworld contact, told her they were heading to Saltmarsh, before disappearing.

She sometimes appears to talk to herself, while she actually talks to some unknown shadow creatures.
